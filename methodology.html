Methodology

How the tracker scores state electricity markets

The Electricity Act 2023 created opportunities for states to establish and regulate their own electricity markets. We assess all 36 states and the FCT across 17 indicators and five pillars, tracking progress from policy formulation to infrastructure and electricity access.

Scoring

Each of the 36 states and the FCT is assessed against the same 17 indicators. A state's score is the number of indicators it meets, out of 17. The ranking shows that score as the share of the 17 indicators met.

Indicators are grouped into five pillars, so each state profile also shows how many indicators it meets within each pillar.

The five pillars

From a state's legal framework to what reaches people on the ground.

Pillar 13 indicators

Policy formulation

A state electricity law, passed and aligned with the national framework.

Pillar 23 indicators

Regulatory setup

A state commission with tariff and licensing frameworks in place.

Pillar 33 indicators

Infrastructure

Generation, transmission and renewable projects on the ground.

Pillar 43 indicators

Market participation

Licensed generation, transmission and renewable companies entering.

Pillar 55 indicators

Performance

Grid reliance falling, access rising, clean transport taking hold.
